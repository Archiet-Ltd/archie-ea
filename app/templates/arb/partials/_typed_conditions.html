A count is shown instead; * no invented due date and no invented `pending` state — the read model reports what is persisted, and an absent due date is an em dash; * no green posture while a blocking condition is unresolved. THE CAPTURE/SUBMIT HAZARD (lane 3 contract). Capture and submit are two commands and capture deliberately does NOT advance the condition: POST /arb/api/conditions//evidence -> condition_evidence_id POST /arb/api/conditions//evidence//submit (NO body) If capture succeeds and submit then fails, re-running capture silently creates a SECOND evidence record.
