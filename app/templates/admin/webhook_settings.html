How to verify a delivery

Every delivery carries Entelim-Webhook-Id, Entelim-Event-Id, Entelim-Event-Type, Entelim-Timestamp and Entelim-Signature: t=<timestamp>,v1=<hex>.

Compute HMAC-SHA256(secret, "<timestamp>." + the exact request body bytes) as lowercase hex and compare it to v1 with a constant-time comparison. Refuse the delivery if the timestamp is more than five minutes from your clock.

Deliveries for one subscription arrive in event order. A delivery that fails is retried with growing delays for 24 hours, then marked dead; you can redeliver it or replay from a sequence number once your receiver is back. Use the sequence number to ignore a delivery you have already handled.
