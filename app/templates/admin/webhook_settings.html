Microsoft Teams

  1. Open the channel you want to post notifications to.
  2. Choose the three dots, then Connectors, and search for Incoming Webhook.
  3. Choose Configure, name it, then Create.
  4. Copy the generated address into the Webhook URL field above.

Slack

  1. Open api.slack.com/apps and create or select your app.
  2. Under Features, turn on Incoming Webhooks.
  3. Choose Add New Webhook to Workspace and pick a channel.
  4. Copy the Webhook URL into the field above.

Payload formats

Teams subscriptions send Adaptive Card messages with a bold event title, a field list and a timestamp. Slack subscriptions send Block Kit messages with a header, a text section and a timestamp. Generic subscriptions send the raw event JSON.
