{% call ui.card() %} {% call ui.card_header() %} {{ ui.card_title(text='How it works') }} {% endcall %} {% call ui.card_content(class='space-y-4') %}
  1. A Teams meeting ends and Microsoft Graph sends a callRecord change notification to the Notification URL above.
  2. Entelim fetches the meeting transcript through the Graph API.
  3. The AI Architect analyses the transcript against the live portfolio and extracts architectural decisions, application names, risk mentions and follow-up actions.
  4. Findings are pushed as in-app notifications to enterprise architects and platform admins. No polling is needed.

Transcription must be enabled for the tenant, and the meeting organiser must turn it on during the call for a transcript to exist.
