Billing questions

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. An organisation administrator cancels from the billing page, or by contacting us. Cancelling stops the subscription from renewing. You keep your plan until the end of the period you have paid for, and you can withdraw a scheduled cancellation before then.

What happens when I add or remove seats?

On Team you pay for editors, and people who only ask questions are free. An organisation administrator changes the number of editor seats, or switches plan, from the billing page at any time.

How are tax and VAT handled?

Any tax due is shown on the payment page before you pay. Add your tax number in the billing portal and it is printed on every invoice issued from then on.

Where do I find my invoices?

Each payment produces an invoice, listed on your organisation's billing page. The invoice e-mail address and billing details are yours to set there.

What happens to my data if I move to a smaller plan?

If you have more people than the new plan admits, everyone keeps access, and no one new can be added until you are within the limit.

Can I pay monthly instead of annually?

Yes. Use the toggle above. Annual billing costs ten months for twelve: $490/year for Startup and $290/editor/year for Team.

What if I already self-host Entelim?

Move to a hosted plan whenever you want to stop running it yourself, and import your model from files. Keep self-hosting for as long as you like: it stays free. If you want Entelim run for you on dedicated infrastructure, talk to us about Enterprise.
