{# Provenance components — the difference between a measured fact, a gap in the record, and an AI interpretation, made visible. Entelim is a system of record. Its hardest UI problem is not showing numbers, it is making sure a reader can never mistake one *kind* of statement for another: a count that came from a column, a count of records that do not carry that column, and a sentence a language model wrote. Before this file the codebase had a first-class primitive for only one of the three — the `|dash` filter for "not computed" — while AI provenance was a copy-pasted `bg-primary/10 text-primary` pill repeated across ~8 templates and "measured" was the unmarked default. Unmarked-by-default is exactly the failure mode: the reader assumes measurement because nothing said otherwise. Three kinds, three visual treatments, one vocabulary: measured neutral a COUNT over the named column. `source` is the column. missing warning records that exist but do not carry the field. ai primary generated text. Always advisory, never a fact. Token note: the tints use `-emphasis` text tokens, per DESIGN.md's rule that `text-warning-foreground` is pure black in both themes and composites to 1.19:1 on `bg-warning/10` in dark mode. #} {% macro provenance_badge(kind) %} {# A single-word claim about where a value came from.
