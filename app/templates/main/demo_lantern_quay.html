About Lantern Quay Systems

Lantern Quay Systems designs and manufactures modular water-monitoring equipment for utilities, environmental agencies and industrial operators. Headquartered on the south coast, the company operates five specialist units — Fabrication, Fulfilment, Field Service, Circular Recovery and Shared Services — each responsible for a distinct stage of the product lifecycle, from precision assembly through to in-field calibration and end-of-life recovery.

What we make

Sensor Modules

Multi-parameter probes measuring turbidity, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, pH and temperature in a single submersible housing. Each module is calibrated against reference standards traceable to national metrology institutes.

Field Testers

Handheld instruments for spot-checking and in-situ calibration verification. Ruggedised for field use with an IP68 rating and a 72-hour offline operating window.

Data Gateways

Edge devices that aggregate readings from up to 500 concurrent sensor modules and relay them to the central event backbone over cellular or satellite links.

Calibration Rigs

Automated calibration stations that verify and adjust sensor modules against certified reference standards, producing an immutable calibration record for every batch.

How we operate

Fabrication

Precision assembly of sensor modules, calibration rigs and gateway hardware. Every unit is tested against reference standards before it leaves the fabrication floor.

Fulfilment

Order processing, packaging and dispatch. The unit manages a calibrated-batch release process that ensures every shipment carries a verifiable calibration chain.

Field Service

On-site installation, calibration verification and preventive maintenance. Engineers carry handheld testers and work from schedules generated by the Field Service Scheduler.

Circular Recovery

End-of-life returns, decontamination, inspection and recertification. Recovered assemblies are reinspected and, where they meet specification, returned to the fulfilment pipeline.

Shared Services

Central functions — IT, finance, procurement, compliance and workforce planning — that support all four operational units. The Quay Compute Pool and Event Relay run from here.

Our technology

The estate runs on a shared compute pool — the Quay Compute Pool — and an event backbone called the Event Relay. Every sensor reading, calibration result and batch release flows through the relay, giving every unit a single, consistent view of the product lifecycle.

Key applications include the Calibration Ledger (the system of record for every calibration event), the Dispatch Planner (which schedules shipments against the calibrated-batch release process), the Sensor Data Hub (which ingests and analyses field readings), and the Field Service Scheduler (which routes engineers to preventive maintenance visits).
