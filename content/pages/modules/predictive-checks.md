# Predictive checks

*Early access: five checks that flag trouble in your model, with the evidence.*

## What Entelim is building

Entelim is building five checks that run over the links in your model and raise a flag before the problem costs you:

- an objective with no delivery mapped to it;
- a project that costs more than it returns;
- one person who owns too much;
- an exposed path to personal data with no control on it;
- a service whose cost rises while nothing else moves.

Each flag shows the records that raised it, so you can check it for yourself.

## What you can do today

Record risks to your systems in the [risk register](/modules/risk-register), see what depends on a system in the [twin map](/modules/digital-twin), and weigh an investment's costs and benefits with [investment analysis](/modules/investment-analysis).
