# AI employees

*Coming soon: AI employees that start work on their own, on a schedule or on an event, and propose actions for a person to approve.*

## What Entelim is building

Entelim is building AI employees that start work on their own, on a schedule or on an event such as a merged pull request or a new Jira issue. Each has one written job. Each proposes actions in the customer's tools, and every action is approved by a named person and logged.

### Architecture analyst

Keeps the architecture model current from GitHub and Jira, flags drift weekly, and prepares the pack before each architecture review.

### Portfolio analyst

Watches cost and overlap across applications, and proposes what to keep, consolidate or retire, with the figures.

### Change coordinator

Writes the impact note for each planned change (what it touches, who owns it) before it is approved.

### Risk and compliance assistant

Links risks and controls to the applications they cover, and flags gaps against the frameworks the organisation chose at sign-up.

## What you can do today

[Ask questions](/modules/ai-chat) about your model in plain words and read the answer in chat. The AI chat can record a risk, link an application to a capability, create architecture elements, simulate impact, propose rationalisation and vendors, and submit for architecture review. Every AI-proposed change waits for a person's approval, with an audit log. Answers inside your own AI chat (MCP) are also available. The model is built from GitHub, Jira and the company website.
