# Scenarios

*Available now: try a change on a copy of your model and compare the scenarios you save.*

## What Scenarios does

Run a change on a copy of the model and see the elements, links, applications, running cost and risks it reaches. Save the run under a name, and compare saved scenarios side by side with a colleague. Your real model is not changed.

## Where to find it

Sign in and open Scenarios under What you are changing.
