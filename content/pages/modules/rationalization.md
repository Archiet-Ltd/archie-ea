# Rationalization

*Duplicate spend, found automatically, with a consolidation list and portfolio KPIs to act on it.*

## What this module does

Two teams both use a project-tracking tool. Three departments each pay for a different survey
platform. Rationalization surfaces exactly that, with a consolidation list to plan the merge and
spend-by-category to show the savings once you act on it.
