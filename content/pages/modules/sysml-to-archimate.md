# Bring your SysML models

*Early access: your systems-engineering model and your enterprise model become one model.*

## What your team gets

Your SysML v2 model comes into the same enterprise model as the applications, owners, costs, risks and compliance that the rest of the business already keeps. Engineers and enterprise architects stop working from two pictures of the same company.

Entelim is opening this to a small group of teams first. Join the waiting list, or talk to us about your models.

## SysML to ArchiMate

Every engineering element will be mapped to an element in [ArchiMate](/modules/architecture-model), so it sits beside everything else you model.

- Blocks and parts will become the nodes, devices, equipment and system software that run your business.
- Requirements will become ArchiMate requirements, linked to the elements that satisfy them.
- Interfaces and ports will become the interfaces your applications and technology use to talk to each other.
- Actors and use cases will become the business actors and services that depend on the system.

Where SysML has no equivalent, ArchiMate will be extended through its own specialisation mechanism, so nothing is dropped. Every element in the enterprise model will name the SysML element it came from, so an auditor can follow a record back to its source.

If you already keep an ArchiMate model, you can [import it today](/use-cases/import-archimate-model).

## MBSE and enterprise architecture in one place

Model-based systems engineering describes the product. Enterprise architecture describes the company that builds, runs and pays for it. They are usually kept apart. Joined, they let you see which applications support a subsystem, who owns it, what it costs, which risks reach it and which compliance obligations apply.

## Ask what a change means

Ask the question the way you would put it to a colleague: what does changing this subsystem mean for our applications, costs, owners and compliance? In early access the answer will come in the [AI chat](/modules/ai-chat) your team already uses, citing the records behind it. The [twin map](/modules/digital-twin) already shows what a failure reaches in the model you have today.

## Requirements you can follow

In early access a requirement will stay connected from the engineer's model to the systems that satisfy it and the evidence that verifies it, and you will follow the trace in either direction.

## What you can do while you wait

[Connect your tools](/connectors) and build the model of your organisation now. The elements from your SysML models will join the same model.
