# Put our business model on one page we can keep current

**For startup founders and early operators.**

## The question

You've drawn the business model canvas once — on a whiteboard, in Miro, in a deck for investors.
Then the company changes and the canvas doesn't, because updating it means opening a tool nobody
else on the team remembers the login for.

## What Entelim answers

A Business Model Canvas built from nine standard blocks, saved per block so updating one box never
means re-entering the whole thing. AI-generated fill suggestions, drawn from your own model data,
get you from blank page to something real faster than typing every box from scratch.

## Before you ask "we already have this in Miro or Notion"

Keep it there. What's different here is that every block gets an AI-drafted suggestion pulled from
your own model data, so you start from something real instead of a blank box.
