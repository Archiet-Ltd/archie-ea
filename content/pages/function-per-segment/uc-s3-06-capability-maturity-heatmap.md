# Show capability maturity as a heat map that never invents a value

**For enterprise architecture teams reporting capability health to the board.**

## The question

A maturity heat map is only useful if the colours mean something. Too many tools fill in an empty
cell with a guess dressed up as data, and the whole report becomes unreliable the moment someone
checks.

## What Entelim is building

A capability heat grid, coloured strictly by what's actually been recorded, over whichever maturity
framework you choose. A cell with nothing behind it stays honestly blank.
