# Show the path from today's estate to the target state, stage by stage

**For enterprise architecture teams building a transformation plan the whole organisation can
follow.**

## The question

A target-state architecture means nothing without the path to get there. Stages, the gaps between
them, and the work closing each gap usually live in three disconnected artefacts that drift apart
the moment one of them changes.

## What Entelim answers

One roadmap page: your stages, the gap register between where you are and where you're going, and
the projects doing the closing — unified, not three separate views quietly disagreeing with each
other.
