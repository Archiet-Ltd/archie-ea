# Which risks sit on our revenue-critical path, and who owns the mitigation?

**For scale-up CTOs and VPs of Engineering.**

## The question

A risk gets logged against one component in a spreadsheet somewhere. What that spreadsheet doesn't
show is whether that component sits on the path your revenue actually depends on.

## What Entelim answers

Ask which risks matter most, and Entelim traces each one's real blast radius across the same
connections the Impact question uses — with the mitigation and its owner recorded alongside it, not
in a separate document.
