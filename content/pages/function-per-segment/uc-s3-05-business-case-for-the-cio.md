# Build the business case for the CIO from the model's own cost and impact figures

**For enterprise architecture teams putting a transformation case in front of the CIO.**

## The question

A CIO doesn't want an opinion — they want capex, opex, three-year TCO, ROI and payback, backed by
numbers that hold up under questioning. Assembling that from scratch, for every initiative, is where
most business cases stall.

## What Entelim answers

A business case linked directly to a capability, initiative or solution, with every financial field
computed from data already in your model — never invented to fill a gap. If a figure doesn't exist
yet, the field says so plainly instead of showing a number nobody can defend.
