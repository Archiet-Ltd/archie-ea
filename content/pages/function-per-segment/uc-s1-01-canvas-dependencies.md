# Which boxes on my canvas depend on things we have not built, hired or signed yet?

**For startup founders whose canvas and whose actual operating reality have quietly drifted apart.**

## The question

Your canvas says you have a payments partner, a fulfilment flow, a key hire. Some of those boxes
are real today. Some are still assumptions nobody's checked in months. The canvas itself can't tell
you which is which.

## What Entelim is building

Every block on your Business Model Canvas linked directly to what you actually run — capabilities,
applications, roles, vendors — so you can ask what breaks or what's at risk straight from a canvas
box, instead of translating it into the model by hand first.

Coming December 2026 to February 2027. Join the waiting list and we'll tell you the day it ships.
