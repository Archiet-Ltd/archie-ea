# See a plausible, clearly generated twin of your own company before the first conversation

**For enterprise architecture teams evaluating Entelim before committing any time to setup.**

## The question

A generic demo tells you what a product can do in theory. Seeing your own company, even a
plausible sketch of it, tells you what it would actually do for you.

## What Entelim is building

Enter your company's website address, and see a generated twin — proposed packs, a draft canvas,
and connector suggestions — clearly marked as a demonstration, never treated as your real data
until you say so.
