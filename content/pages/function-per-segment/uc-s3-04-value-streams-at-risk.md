# Which value streams are at risk because the capabilities under them are immature?

**For enterprise architecture teams and the CIO they report to.**

## The question

A value stream depends on a stack of capabilities underneath it. When one of those capabilities
isn't mature enough, the value stream is at risk — but that connection rarely shows up until
something actually breaks.

## What Entelim answers

Ask which value streams are at risk, and get a trace over your curated capability dependencies,
with the initiatives and success metrics already attached to each one. The risk is visible before
it becomes an incident, not after.
