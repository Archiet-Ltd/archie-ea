# How many connections did the model work out for us, and can I open any one to its proof?

**For enterprise architecture teams who want to trust the model, not just use it.**

## The question

A tool that claims to derive connections automatically is only as trustworthy as its ability to show
its working. Without a way to check, "the model worked it out" is just a claim.

## What Entelim is building

A derivation yield report per tenant — how many connections were entered directly versus worked out
by the product — alongside a screen where you can open any single derived connection and see exactly
how it was reasoned.
