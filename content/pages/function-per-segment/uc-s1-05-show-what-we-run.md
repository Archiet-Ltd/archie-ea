# Show an investor what we run and who owns it, in an afternoon

**For startup founders preparing for a fundraise, a board meeting, or diligence.**

## The question

A diligence request or a board question lands, and it asks for the operating picture — not the
pitch deck. Pulling that together by hand, from memory and scattered docs, is an afternoon you don't
have during a raise.

## What Entelim answers

Your full application list, your org chart and ownership map, and a generated picture of how it all
connects — together, in one place, read from the same model you're already keeping current for every
other question.
