# Which of my people is a single point of failure?

**For owner-operators and managing directors.**

## The question

A key person resigns, and suddenly nobody can list what they actually held. That gap shows up at
the worst possible moment — usually right after they've already gone.

## What Entelim answers

An accountability answer on Ask, backed by an org chart and RACI — so who owns what is visible
before someone leaves, not scrambled together after.
