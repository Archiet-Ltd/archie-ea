# Bring our LeanIX or Ardoq fact sheets across

**For enterprise architecture teams already running LeanIX or Ardoq who want their existing records
to travel with them.**

## The question

You've spent years building out fact sheets in LeanIX or Ardoq. Re-entering all of it by hand isn't
realistic, and it shouldn't be the price of trying something else.

## What Entelim is building

A direct import from LeanIX and Ardoq exports — no manual re-entry, no intermediate file format to
wrangle. Your fact sheets become elements in your model you can work with immediately.

Coming 2027. Join the waiting list and we'll tell you the day it ships.

In the meantime, importing a model from another architecture tool already works today — see
[Import our existing model](/enterprise-architecture/import).
