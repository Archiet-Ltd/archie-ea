# Bring our LeanIX or Ardoq fact sheets across

**For enterprise architecture teams already running LeanIX or Ardoq who want their existing records
to travel with them.**

## The question

You've spent years building out fact sheets in LeanIX or Ardoq. Re-entering all of it by hand isn't
realistic, and it shouldn't be the price of trying something else.

## The working path today

There's no direct LeanIX or Ardoq import. The working path is to export your model to ArchiMate Open
Exchange format or CSV, then bring it across — see
[Import our existing model](/use-cases/import-archimate-model).
