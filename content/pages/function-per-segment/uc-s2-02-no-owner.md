# Which systems have no owner, and which owner is a single point of failure?

**For scale-up CTOs and VPs of Engineering.**

## The question

A SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audit asks for an asset inventory with owners attached. Half the systems in a
typical scale-up either have no recorded owner, or one person owns so much that they're a risk in
their own right.

## What Entelim is building

An accountability answer on Ask: ownership joined to organisation unit, next to the Org Chart and
RACI view, showing plainly where no owner is recorded instead of guessing one.
