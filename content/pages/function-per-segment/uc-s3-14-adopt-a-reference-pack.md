# Adopt the standard shape of what we run on day one and map our own systems onto it

**For enterprise architecture teams standardising on a platform.**

## The question

Adopting a platform like SAP S/4HANA or ServiceNow means modelling the same standard shape every
other customer has already modelled. Doing that from a blank page, every time, is wasted effort.

## What Entelim is building

Curated reference packs for major platforms, adopted through a guided questionnaire. Map your own
CMDB, Jira, or spreadsheet data onto the pack's elements through a review queue that flags anything
uncertain — with version upgrades that preserve your own customisations.

Coming December 2026 to February 2027. Join the waiting list and we'll tell you the day it ships.
