# Give the acquirer or the auditor a current architecture picture we did not draw by hand

**For scale-up CTOs facing a funding round, an audit, or acquisition diligence.**

## The question

Diligence or an audit asks for the system landscape. The diagram that answers it either doesn't
exist, or was drawn six months ago by someone who's left the company.

## What Entelim answers

Ask a question, and get a twin map generated directly from the answer — current because it reads
the live model, not redrawn from memory. Save views to a library, jump straight from any element to
its exact place in the diagram, and export the whole picture for whoever's on the other side of the
table.
