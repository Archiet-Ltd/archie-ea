# Pick the products you use and see what breaks if one fails, before typing anything

**For startup founders and early operators who don't want to type their stack in by hand.**

## The question

You use maybe fifteen tools. Typing each one in, one at a time, before Entelim can tell you anything
useful is exactly the kind of setup tax that makes a five-person team give up on a tool before it
ever answers a real question.

## What Entelim is building

A reference-pack picker on the second onboarding screen: pick the products you actually use from a
list written in the vendor's own words, and Entelim creates the right elements for you — with
provenance attached, so you can always see they came from a pack, not something you typed. The
Impact question then answers over all of it at once, immediately.

Coming December 2026 to February 2027. Join the waiting list and we'll tell you the day it ships.

## Who this is for

Founders and early operators putting the business on one page for the first time — the same person
asking what breaks if a single cloud platform or payment provider goes down. This is the step before
that question: fewer things to type, so Entelim has something to answer from day one.
