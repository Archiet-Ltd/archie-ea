# Show me what I own and what depends on it, so I know what I am on the hook for

**For engineering managers and staff engineers, not just the CTO signing the contract.**

## The question

You're accountable for a service. What you don't always have is a clean, personal view of exactly
what that includes — separate from the whole company's estate, which is someone else's problem to
navigate.

## What Entelim answers

A personal dashboard scoped to what you own, with health status for each application, so your slice
of the estate is always one click away instead of a filtered search through everyone else's.
