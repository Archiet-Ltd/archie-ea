# Tell me plainly what you could read from our site and what only we can tell you

**For owner-operators who want a straight line between "read from your website" and "you told us."**

## The question

A tool that reads your public website and starts making confident claims about your systems, your
people, or your contracts is guessing — and guessing dressed up as insight is worse than no insight
at all.

## What Entelim is building

A clear boundary built into onboarding: what was read from your own pages, shown with the exact
page it came from, versus what only you can tell us — with a plain next step for filling in each
gap yourself.
