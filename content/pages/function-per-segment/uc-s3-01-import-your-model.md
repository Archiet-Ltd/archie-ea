# Import our existing Archi or Open Exchange model and keep working

**For enterprise architecture teams with years of modelling already done.**

## The question

You didn't start from nothing. Years of ArchiMate work already exist in Archi, or as an Open
Exchange export from whatever tool you've been using. Starting over is not an option, and it
shouldn't have to be.

## What Entelim answers

Import your model directly — ArchiMate Open Exchange and .archimate files both bring elements,
relationships and properties across intact, with import history so you can see exactly what came in
and when. Everything lands in one element browser, across every ArchiMate layer, ready to work with.
