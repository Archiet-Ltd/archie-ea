# What must be true for the revenue stream we are betting on, and which of it is not ready?

**For startup founders betting the company on one thing working.**

## The question

You've picked the revenue stream you're betting on. What you haven't necessarily mapped is
everything that has to actually hold up underneath it — the capabilities, the systems, the people —
before that bet pays off.

## What Entelim answers

Ask which value streams are at risk, and Entelim traces the capabilities underneath each one to find
where maturity isn't where it needs to be — before that gap becomes the reason the bet doesn't pay
off.
