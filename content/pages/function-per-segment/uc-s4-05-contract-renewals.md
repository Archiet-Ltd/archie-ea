# Which contracts renew soon, and what depends on them?

**For owner-operators and managing directors running a services business.**

## The question

A contract auto-renews next month, and nobody's checked in a year whether you still need what it
provides, or what would actually break if you cancelled it.

## What Entelim answers

Every contract, its renewal date, its licence entitlements, and the vendor behind it — plus a direct
line into what happens if that vendor lets you down, from the same Impact question that answers
every other "what happens if" you ask.
