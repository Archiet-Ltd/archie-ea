# Changelog

This page lists planned changes by quarter. The pages that describe Entelim describe what it does
today; anything not yet in the product is listed here and nowhere else.

## Q4 2026

- Save, share, subscribe to and export an answer, as a spreadsheet or a PDF.
- Cited, shareable answers to organisation-wide questions.
- A token-secured public API with developer kits.
- Tracing of capability ownership and goals to what realises them.
- A named owner on every application, with owners and health shown in every impact answer.
- Identities kept through renames when a model is re-imported.
- Ownership across the whole model.

## Q1 2027

- Connections to the tools you already use, a replay and event catalogue for webhooks, and
  connectors that write through one approval queue, starting with ServiceNow and your identity provider.
- Read access for your own AI assistants through the open Model Context Protocol.
- Service mapping and reconciliation with your configuration records.
- Stage evidence and gate conformance.
- Lineage across every hop, with sensitivity carried forward to every consumer.
- Risk propagated through every dependency.
- A control library with evidence that proves each control operates.
- Renewal alerts that show the last day to cancel.
- An AI system register with a proposed EU AI Act risk class for each system.
- Insights raised without being asked.

## Q2 2027

- One home for every cost, totalled in your reporting currency.
- Objectives and key results with measured results.
- Benefits tracked to measured outcomes.
- Data quality rules and trends.
- Incidents, changes, telemetry and service levels on the model.
- Licence usage that is measured rather than typed in.
- AI usage and cost by provider.

## Q3 2027

- Scenario branches with every assumption visible, and what-if forecasts.
- Skills and capacity planning.
- Forecasting on top of the model.

## Q4 2027

- Portfolio and roadmap optimisation.

## Q1 2028

- A model that keeps itself current within limits you set.

## Through 2027

- Financial what-if and budget-constrained portfolio optimisation.
- Model health findings and design-to-delivery traceability.
- Appetite, key risk indicators and scenario-based resilience tests.
- Forecasts and recommendations tracked to outcomes.
- Multi-step agent workflows.
- Comments and reviews on shared answers.
