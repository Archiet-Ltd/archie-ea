# Startup and scale-up CTOs first, then the roles around them

Entelim starts with the person who runs engineering at a company with no architecture team. Connect GitHub,
see what you run and who owns it, and ask the AI chat what breaks if a service fails. Every answer cites the
records behind it. As the company grows, the same model serves the roles below.

## Startup and scale-up CTOs

What do we run, and where did it come from? What breaks if this system fails? What are we paying for? Can I
trust what the model says?
