# Frameworks and canvases Entelim supports

Choose the frameworks and canvases your company works with while you sign up, and Entelim loads them for
you. Each framework is adopted with its own list: phases, layers, states, practices, objectives or controls.
Each canvas is created once and filled from the elements already in your model, so the same customer
segment, goal or application is one element however many canvases show it.

Every page below says what the framework or canvas is, how Entelim loads it, how to use it, why Entelim
is different and answers the questions people ask. No page claims that using Entelim makes a company
certified or compliant; those decisions belong to auditors and regulators.
