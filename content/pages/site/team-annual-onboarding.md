# Team annual plan with onboarding: enterprise architecture tool onboarding

Enterprise architecture tool onboarding is the work of getting a new tool loaded with your real landscape and your team able to keep it current. The Team annual plan with onboarding gives you the Team plan at $290/editor/year plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding engagement. In it we build your first real model of your landscape with you, import your existing data, and train your team to keep the model up to date on its own.

## Who it is for

The Team plan is for scale-ups and services companies of 50 to 500 people. This offer suits you if:

- you have decided to use Entelim and want the model loaded and working, not left for someone to find time for;
- your landscape lives in spreadsheets, an Archi file or exports from another tool, and nobody has had the hours to bring it together;
- you want your own people to run the model afterwards, and not depend on us.

If you are not sure yet, start on the free Community plan, or buy the [architecture health check](/architecture-health-check) first. The health check is a fixed-price assessment that gives you a report and a model to build on.

## What you get

**The Team plan, committed annually.** Entelim's Team plan is priced per editor, and people who only ask questions are free. An editor is a member of your workspace who is not set to read-only. Team adds single sign-on, the architecture review board workflow, and your own model key for the AI features. Every plan includes every question Entelim answers, the twin map, the Business Model Canvas and file import.

**Onboarding, as working sessions.** Onboarding is working sessions with your people, not a generic webinar. It has three parts:

- **Your first real model.** We build your organisation's first model of its landscape together with you: the applications, the capabilities they support, the technology under them and the dependencies between them, so that a question like what breaks if a system fails has an answer from day one.
- **Import of your existing data.** We bring in what you already have: spreadsheets (CSV and Excel), Archi files and ArchiMate Open Exchange files, and exports from your current tools. Imports keep a history, so you can see what came in and when.
- **Training to keep it current.** We teach your team to add and change applications, set owners and lifecycle stages, record costs, and read the answers, so that the model stays true after we leave.

## How it works

1. **Request the offer below.** We set up a short scoping call.
2. **Scoping call.** We agree which data to import, who will edit the model, how many editors you need, the sessions and a start date.
3. **Set-up.** Your workspace is set up on the Team plan and your editors are added.
4. **Import.** We load your existing data and check what came in against your own records.
5. **Working sessions.** We build the first model with your team and train them as we go.
6. **Handover.** Your team runs the model. You keep the workspace, the model and the plan.

## What we need from you

- **Your editors.** The people who will keep the model current. They count towards the price. People who only ask questions do not.
- **Your existing data.** Spreadsheets, Archi files, Open Exchange files or exports. A list of applications is enough to begin.
- **Time from your team for the working sessions,** and one person who can make decisions about what the model should cover.

## Price and terms

- **$290/editor/year,** billed annually. This is the Team plan at the annual rate, which is the $29/editor/month rate for ten months instead of twelve.
- **$1,500, one time,** for onboarding.

For example, a team with ten editors pays $2,900 for the year plus $1,500 for onboarding. People who only ask questions are free and do not change the figure.

Both prices are in US dollars and match the [pricing page](/pricing). We confirm invoicing and payment terms when we agree the start date. The onboarding is one time and does not recur.

## How this differs from the architecture health check

The [architecture health check](/architecture-health-check) is a $4,500 assessment: we run Entelim over your data and give you a written report. The Team annual plan with onboarding is for teams who want to run Entelim themselves: you get the plan, a model built with you and the training to keep it up to date.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does enterprise architecture tool onboarding cost?

The Team annual plan with onboarding costs $290 per editor per year plus a one-time $1,500 for onboarding. People who only ask questions are free. A team with ten editors pays $2,900 for the year plus $1,500 for onboarding.

### How many sessions are included?

Onboarding is working sessions with your team, for a fixed $1,500. We agree the sessions and the start date on the scoping call, once we know how much data you have and how many people will edit the model.

### Can we start monthly and switch to annual later?

This offer is the annual plan with onboarding. If you want to try Team month by month first, the monthly price is $29 per editor. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for every plan and for what each includes.

### What if we already use Archi?

Then your model comes with you. Entelim imports Archi files and ArchiMate Open Exchange files, bringing across elements, relationships and properties, and keeps an import history. Onboarding includes importing them and checking the result with your team.

### What counts as an editor?

An editor is a member of your workspace who is not set to read-only. Editors can change the model. Read-only members can ask questions and see answers, and they are free on the Team plan, so only the people who keep the model current count towards the price.

### Is there a cheaper way to start?

Yes. The Community plan is free with no time limit, for one organisation and up to three people. Startup is $49 a month for up to ten people. You can also self-host Entelim for free under AGPL-3.0. This offer is for teams that want help getting set up.
