# Pricing

Enterprise Intelligence Management priced per company, not per application: free for up to three people,
$49 a month for a team of ten, $29 per editor a month for larger teams with people who only ask questions
free, and Enterprise from $24,000 a year. Self-host the open-source edition free.

Startups run on the free plan or on Startup: the business model canvas, the value streams behind your
revenue and the twin map, set up in minutes. Companies of 50 to 500 people move to Team, where owners,
architects[[if live PG-GOV]] and the review board[[/if]] work in one model and everyone else asks questions at no cost.

Every plan lists only what works today. Capabilities in early access, such as the
[control library](/early-access/control-library), [renewal alerts](/early-access/renewal-alerts-and-licence-usage)
and the [AI system register](/early-access/ai-systems-register), are not part of any plan yet: open one to join its
waiting list, or see the [features page](/features) for the rest. When one opens it reaches Startup and Team at no
extra charge.
