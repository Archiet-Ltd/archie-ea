# Pricing

Enterprise Intelligence Management priced per company, not per application: free for up to three people,
$49 a month for a team of ten, $29 per editor a month (minimum five editors) for larger teams with people who only view
free, and Enterprise from $24,000 a year. Self-host the open-source edition free.

Startups run on the free plan or on Startup: your company profile, your applications and their owners, and one
overview, set up in one sitting. Companies of 50 to 500 people move to Team, where everyone who only views is free.
Paying for Startup and Team opens soon; join the paid-plan waiting list, and start on Community today.
