# Features

Entelim is one model with modules over it, not a bundle of separate tools that happen to share a
login. Everything below reads and writes the same twin, so an answer in one module carries evidence
from every other.

## Model and diagrams

- [Architecture Model](/modules/architecture-model) — every element of your architecture, at every
  layer, in one browser.
- [Diagrams & Composer](/modules/diagrams) — saved diagram views, with a composer that jumps
  straight to any element's exact place in the model.
- [Batch Import](/modules/batch-import) — spreadsheets and full architecture exports, landing in the
  same model.
- [Industry Reference Frameworks](/modules/industry-apqc) — an APQC-style process model that seeds
  your capability map, so you never start from a blank page.

## Applications and portfolio

- [Applications](/modules/applications) — every application Entelim knows about, where it came
  from, and who owns it.
- [My Applications](/modules/my-applications) — not the whole portfolio, just what you personally
  own, and what's on the hook because of it.
- [Portfolio](/modules/portfolio) — capabilities, value streams, programmes and work packages,
  joined in one view.
- [Rationalization](/modules/rationalization) — duplicate spend, found automatically, with a
  consolidation list and portfolio KPIs to act on it.
- [Duplicate Detection](/modules/duplicate-detection) — the engine behind Rationalization: finds two
  applications doing the same job before you go looking for them.

## Risk, compliance and maturity

- [Risk Register](/modules/risk-register) — every recorded risk, traced across what it touches, to
  show its real blast radius, not just the obvious one.
- [Compliance Frameworks](/modules/compliance-frameworks) — checks every application in your model
  against each framework's controls, showing what's implemented and what's still a gap.
- [Capability Maturity](/modules/capability-maturity) — a heat grid that shows maturity where
  someone recorded it, and shows missing as missing, never a guess.
- [Gap Analysis](/modules/gap-analysis) — what's missing between today's estate and the target
  state you've defined.
- [Architecture Review Board](/modules/arb) — reviews, sessions and decisions, with the impact
  evidence already attached before the meeting.

## Planning and delivery

- [Roadmaps](/modules/roadmaps) — stages, the gap timeline between them, and the projects that
  close each gap, on one page.
- [Projects](/modules/projects) — dates, status, and estimated versus actual cost for every
  project.
- [Business Case](/modules/business-case) — capex, opex, three-year TCO, ROI and payback, computed
  from figures already in your model.
- [Investment Analysis](/modules/investment-analysis) — every business case rolled up into one
  portfolio-wide view of cost and priority.
- [Business Model Canvas](/modules/business-model-canvas) — nine standard blocks, saved per block,
  with AI fill suggestions drawn from your own model data.
- [Value Streams](/modules/value-streams) — each revenue stream, and the capabilities underneath it
  that have to work for it to run.

## People, vendors and spend

- [Org Chart & RACI](/modules/org-chart) — who owns what, honestly, including the systems that
  don't have an owner recorded.
- [Vendors](/modules/vendors) — every supplier and provider behind your applications, in one list.
- [Procurement](/modules/procurement) — contracts, renewals, licence entitlements, spend by
  category, and compliance status.
- [Solutions](/modules/solutions) — every solution design in one list: the record the review board
  actually reviews.

## AI and integrations

- [AI Chat](/modules/ai-chat) — one chat interface for your AI assistant to query the model
  directly, instead of guessing at an answer.
- [Integrations](/modules/integrations) — every connector in one place, with a health page showing
  exactly what's actually feeding your model.
