# The eighteen capabilities of Enterprise Intelligence Management

Enterprise Intelligence Management is one living, explainable model of your enterprise, turned into
answers, insights, recommendations and simulations. Entelim delivers it through eighteen
capabilities, grouped into five questions every enterprise asks: set direction, know what you run,
stay in control, get answers before you need them, and connect and trust.

## Set direction

*Where are we going, and is the money following the plan?*

<h3 id="strategy-management">Strategy Management</h3>

Turn what you intend into goals, objectives and the capabilities that deliver them, and see whether
the strategy is actually being carried out.

**Today:** map your value streams stage by stage to the capabilities behind them, see which value
streams are at risk, lay out drivers and goals in a motivation model, and capture the business model
on one canvas.

**Next:** capability ownership and goals traced to what realises them follow in Q4 2026; objectives
and key results with measured results arrive in Q2 2027.

Delivered today by: [Business Model Canvas](/modules/business-model-canvas),
[Value Streams at Risk](/use-cases/value-streams-at-risk).

<h3 id="portfolio-management">Portfolio Management</h3>

Keep one current portfolio of applications, technology, products and initiatives, each with an
owner, a health picture and a disposition you can defend with data.

**Today:** keep your application inventory with its interfaces and dependencies, place technology on
a radar, score applications for rationalisation and work from one consolidation list.

**Next:** a named owner on every application, and owners and health on every impact answer, from
Q4 2026.

Delivered today by: [Applications](/modules/applications), [Portfolio](/modules/portfolio),
[Rationalization](/modules/rationalization), [What I Own](/use-cases/what-i-own).

<h3 id="transformation-management">Transformation Management</h3>

Plan change from where you are to where you want to be, through gaps, roadmaps, programmes and work
packages, and prove the outcome when it lands.

**Today:** record gaps, roadmaps, programmes and work packages in the same model as the estate they
change, and ask what any programme touches.

**Next:** stage evidence and gate conformance in Q1 2027; benefits tracked to measured outcomes in
Q2 2027.

Delivered today by: [Roadmaps](/modules/roadmaps), [Programme Tracking](/use-cases/programme-tracking).

<h3 id="investment-and-financial-management">Investment and Financial Management</h3>

Give every cost one home in one reporting currency, appraise what to fund, and tie spend to the
capabilities and outcomes it pays for.

**Today:** build a business case with net present value against the applications and capabilities it
affects.

**Next:** one home for every cost, totalled in your reporting currency, arrives in Q2 2027; financial
what-if in Q3 2027; budget-constrained portfolio optimisation in Q4 2027.

Delivered today by: [Business Case](/modules/business-case).

## Know what you run

*What does the business actually run on, and who owns it?*

<h3 id="architecture-management">Architecture Management</h3>

Model the whole enterprise across business, applications, data and technology in ArchiMate 3.2,
design solutions against your standards, and keep the model healthy.

**Today:** import an existing model from an Open Exchange file, an Archi file or a spreadsheet, edit
it in the composer, browse every view in the diagram library, see it all on the twin map, and record
architecture decisions.

**Next:** lossless re-import that keeps identities through renames from Q4 2026; model health
findings and design-to-delivery traceability through 2027.

Delivered today by: [Architecture Model](/modules/architecture-model),
[Diagrams and Composer](/modules/diagrams).

<h3 id="data-management">Data Management</h3>

Know what data you hold, where each piece is mastered, how it moves from system to system and who
looks after it.

**Today:** keep an enterprise data model, trace lineage, assign stewards and declare the system of
record for each master data domain.

**Next:** lineage across every hop and sensitivity carried forward to every consumer in Q1 2027; data
quality rules and trends in Q2 2027.

<h3 id="people-and-organisation-management">People and Organisation Management</h3>

Connect people, teams, roles and skills to the capabilities and systems they own and run, so every
question has a name attached.

**Today:** keep your organisation chart with roles and responsibilities, and map your stakeholders.

**Next:** ownership across the model from Q4 2026; skills and capacity planning from Q3 2027.

Delivered today by: [Org Chart and RACI](/modules/org-chart).

<h3 id="operations-and-service-management">Operations and Service Management</h3>

Bring the live state of the business into the model (services, incidents, changes, service levels
and telemetry) so the twin reflects what is happening now, not what was drawn last quarter.

This is where the twin goes live. Service mapping and reconciliation with your configuration records
start in Q1 2027, and incidents, changes, telemetry and service levels on the model arrive in
Q2 2027.

## Stay in control

*What could hurt us, what must we prove, and what are we committed to?*

<h3 id="risk-management">Risk Management</h3>

Hold one risk register, see risk that travels through dependencies rather than only the risk written
on an element, and measure exposure against your appetite.

**Today:** keep the risk register and ask which risks sit on the path to any element.

**Next:** risk propagated through every dependency from Q1 2027; appetite and key risk indicators in
Q2 2027; resilience and concentration tests by scenario from Q3 2027.

Delivered today by: [Risk Register](/modules/risk-register).

<h3 id="compliance-and-governance">Compliance and Governance</h3>

Map obligations to controls and evidence, run architecture review and gates, and give auditors
complete evidence without a scramble.

**Today:** run an architecture review board from submission to decision, record conditions and
waivers, define governance gates, monitor policy, handle data subject requests and query, export and
verify the audit log.

**Next:** a control library with evidence that proves each control operates, from Q1 2027.

Delivered today by: [Architecture Review Board](/modules/arb),
[Compliance Frameworks](/modules/compliance-frameworks).

<h3 id="vendor-and-procurement-management">Vendor and Procurement Management</h3>

Know every supplier, contract and licence, what each one serves and what it costs, and never miss
the last day to cancel.

**Today:** keep vendors, contracts, licences, renewals and spend in one procurement workspace tied to
the applications they serve.

**Next:** renewal alerts with the last day to cancel in Q1 2027; licence usage measured, not typed, in
Q2 2027.

Delivered today by: [Vendors](/modules/vendors), [Procurement](/modules/procurement).

<h3 id="ai-management">AI Management</h3>

Inventory, classify, evaluate and govern every AI system in the enterprise, including Entelim's own,
and manage what AI costs you.

**Today:** Entelim governs its own AI the way you will govern yours: every action its assistant takes
passes one permission check, writes wait for approval, and its agents are listed in a register you
can read.

**Next:** your own AI system register, with a proposed EU AI Act risk class for each system, opens in
Q1 2027; AI usage and cost by provider follow in Q2 2027.

## Get answers before you need them

*What does it mean, what happens next, and what should we do?*

<h3 id="enterprise-intelligence">Enterprise Intelligence</h3>

Ask the model a question and get an answer that cites its sources, explains itself and shows what it
does not know. Then let the platform raise what matters before you ask.

**Today:** pick any element and ask four questions: what breaks if it fails, which strategy it
serves, which programmes touch it and which risks reach it. Every row shows its source and how it was
derived, and you can see the model as it stood on any past date.

**Next:** organisation-wide questions with cited, shareable answers in Q4 2026; insights raised
without being asked in Q1 2027; forecasts from Q3 2027; recommendations tracked to measured outcomes
from Q4 2027.

Delivered today by: [What breaks if this fails, and who gets called](/use-cases/what-breaks-and-who-gets-called).

<h3 id="simulation-and-scenario-planning">Simulation and Scenario Planning</h3>

Branch the model, change it, and see what happens to cost, risk, resilience and capacity before you
commit money.

Scenario branches with every assumption visible arrive in Q3 2027, with portfolio and roadmap
optimisation in Q4 2027. Until then, the impact question shows exactly what one failure would break.

<h3 id="agentic-automation">Agentic Automation</h3>

Let governed agents and your own AI assistants read the model, propose changes and, within limits you
delegate, act on it, with one permission check, one approval queue and a full audit trail.

**Today:** the in-product assistant works through the same permission check and approval inbox every
agent will use.

**Next:** your own AI assistants read the model securely in Q1 2027; multi-step agent workflows in
Q4 2027; a model that maintains itself within limits you set in Q1 2028.

Delivered today by: [AI Chat](/modules/ai-chat).

## Connect and trust

*Does it fit how we work, and can we trust it with our data?*

<h3 id="collaboration-and-communication">Collaboration and Communication</h3>

Make every answer shareable, reviewable and deliverable, and give every person one place for their
work.

**Today:** diagrams and the audit trail export now.

**Next:** answers you can save, share, subscribe to and export from Q4 2026; comments and reviews
follow in 2027.

<h3 id="integration-and-interoperability">Integration and Interoperability</h3>

Open the model to the rest of your stack through a secure API, events and connectors, with every
incoming change matched by identifier and approved before it lands.

**Today:** import ArchiMate Open Exchange, Archi and spreadsheet files, and send signed event
notifications to Slack, Microsoft Teams or any webhook.

**Next:** a token-secured public API with developer kits in Q4 2026; connectors that write through
one approval queue, starting with ServiceNow and your identity provider, in Q1 2027.

Delivered today by: [Architecture Model](/modules/architecture-model) (file import).

<h3 id="administration-and-trust">Administration and Trust</h3>

Enterprise sign-in, access control, proven tenant isolation, audit and billing, so the platform
passes the checks your security team runs first.

**Today:** single sign-on through SAML or OpenID Connect, every record scoped to your organisation, a
verifiable audit log, and plans you can buy, change and cancel yourself.

**Next:** isolation proven on every route by an independent test in Q4 2026; a SOC 2 Type I report in
Q2 2027; EU data residency in Q3 2027; SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 in Q4 2027.
