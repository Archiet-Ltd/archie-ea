# Architecture health check: a fixed-price enterprise architecture assessment

The architecture health check is a fixed-price enterprise architecture assessment of your application landscape. For $4,500 we import your own data into Entelim, run its analysis over it, and deliver a written report within two weeks of kickoff. The report covers what is in your landscape, what breaks if each critical system fails and who owns it, what you pay for twice, which applications have no owner, and which contracts renew next.

## Who it is for

Buy the health check when someone is about to ask you a question about your systems and you cannot answer it from a list.

- **A CIO or head of architecture** before a budget round, an audit, an acquisition or a decision about which tools to keep.
- **A scale-up CTO** before a funding round, when an investor will ask what you run and what depends on what.
- **A programme or portfolio lead** who has been handed a landscape nobody has written down and needs a first, honest picture of it.

You do not need an existing model. You need a list of your applications, in whatever form it exists today.

## What is included

The health check runs the analyses Entelim already performs, over your real data and not a demonstration. Each is a section of the report.

- **Application inventory.** Every application with its recorded owner, cost, lifecycle stage and business criticality, and the record completeness for each, so you can see which fields are missing and for how many applications.
- **What breaks if a system fails.** For each critical system, the things that would stop, by layer and by how many steps away they are, marked as stated in your data or derived from it, with the recorded owner of each affected element or a plain statement that no owner is recorded.
- **Duplicate and overlapping applications.** Duplicate groups found by running detection across the inventory, and where several applications share a capability, the contracts behind them with their combined value and nearest end date.
- **Applications with no recorded owner.** The list, grouped by business criticality, and the ownership coverage by business unit.
- **Rationalisation scores.** A TIME model recommendation (Tolerate, Invest, Migrate or Eliminate) and a health score from 0 to 100 for each application that has enough data to be scored. Applications without enough evidence are listed as such.
- **Renewals.** Contracts renewing in the next quarter, with the renewal date, annual cost and whether the contract auto-renews, where you give us contracts.
- **A model you keep.** Your landscape stays in Entelim as an ArchiMate model you can keep working in.
- **A read-out call** to walk through the report with your team.

Where your data is incomplete, the report shows the gap as a gap. A missing owner, cost or contract date is reported as missing, never filled with a guess.

## How it works

1. **Scoping call.** A short call to agree what to import and which systems matter most. The clock starts at kickoff.
2. **Import.** You send the files. We load them into Entelim: spreadsheets, an Archi or ArchiMate Open Exchange file, or exports from your current tools.
3. **Analysis.** We run duplicate detection, rationalisation scoring and the dependency analysis across your landscape, and check every finding against your data.
4. **Written report.** The report is delivered within two weeks of kickoff.
5. **Read-out call.** We take your team through the findings and the gaps in the data.

## What we need from you

- **Your application list.** A spreadsheet is enough. Name, owner, cost and lifecycle stage are the fields that make the analysis useful, and any you have are better than none.
- **An existing model, if you have one.** An Archi file, an ArchiMate Open Exchange file, or an export from your current tool.
- **Contracts and licences, if you want the renewals section.** A list with vendor, annual cost, renewal or end date and whether the contract auto-renews.
- **A named person to answer questions** while we load and check the data.

Your side is gathering the files and answering questions about what they mean. Entelim imports CSV and Excel spreadsheets and ArchiMate models directly, so the files do not need reformatting first.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does an architecture health check take?

The report is delivered within two weeks of kickoff. Kickoff follows a short scoping call where we agree what to import. How quickly you can send the files is the main thing that moves the start date, so send them as soon as the call is booked.

### What data do you need?

A list of your applications is enough, as a spreadsheet. Owner, cost and lifecycle stage make the analysis more useful. An existing Archi or ArchiMate Open Exchange model and a list of contracts are optional extras that add sections to the report.

### What if our data is incomplete?

The report shows gaps as gaps. If an application has no owner, no cost or no contract date, the report says so and counts it. Entelim does not guess a value to fill a blank, so a gap in your data becomes a finding you can act on.

### What happens to our data after the report?

The model stays in your Entelim workspace, which you control, and you can export it as an ArchiMate Open Exchange file at any time. Read the [privacy page](/privacy) for how Entelim handles personal data.

### Can we self-host Entelim afterwards?

Yes. Entelim is open source under AGPL-3.0, and self-hosting is free. You can export your model as an ArchiMate Open Exchange file and import it into a self-hosted copy. A commercial licence and supported self-hosting are available if you want Entelim run for you.

### Is the price fixed?

Yes. The health check is $4,500, one time, for the scope above. It is separate from any Entelim plan. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for the plans.
