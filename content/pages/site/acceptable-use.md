# Acceptable use

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

This page sets out how the hosted Entelim service may be used. It sits alongside the [terms](/terms)
and does not change them.

## What is not allowed

- Using Entelim to break the law, or to store or share unlawful content.
- Attacking, overloading or interfering with the service, or trying to get around its security.
- Trying to reach another organisation's data, or any data that is not yours or your organisation's.
- Using the assistant to produce harmful material, such as content meant to deceive, harass or harm
  people, or malicious software.
- Sharing your sign-in with people outside your organisation, or using someone else's account.

## What happens if the rules are broken

Archiet Ltd may suspend an account or an organisation's access to the service while a breach is looked
into, and may end access where a breach is serious or repeated.

## Reporting a problem

If you see something that breaks these rules, or you think your account has been misused, tell us through
the [contact form](/contact).
