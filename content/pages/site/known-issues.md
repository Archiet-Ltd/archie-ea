# Known issues

These are the limits you may meet today, and what to do instead.

- Paid plans are arranged by conversation rather than through a checkout, so use the [contact page](/contact) to start one.
- The implementation planning dashboard is not listed in the menu yet, so plan work from the solution pages instead.
- The integrations page is not listed in the menu yet, so connect sources from the connector pages instead.
- The compliance frameworks dashboard is not listed in the menu yet, so review compliance from each application page.
- The investment priorities and investment matrix pages are not listed in the menu yet, so compare investments from the portfolio views instead.
- The change history page is not listed in the menu yet, so open the history on each item you are looking at.
- The built-in assistant answers only from the data your organisation has entered, so add or import your data first if an answer comes back empty.
