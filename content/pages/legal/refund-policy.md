# Refund policy

This policy explains how cancellation and refunds work for subscriptions to the hosted Entelim
service sold by Archiet Ltd. It forms part of your subscription agreement.

## Who this applies to

Entelim is sold to businesses and other organisations, not to consumers. The statutory 14-day
cancellation period in the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 therefore does not apply to a
subscription. By subscribing, you confirm you are buying for your organisation's business purposes.

## Cancelling

An organisation administrator can cancel at any time from the billing page or by
[contacting us](/contact). Cancelling stops the subscription from renewing. You keep full access
until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you can export your data for 30 days
after it ends.

## When we refund

Fees are paid in advance for each subscription period and are not refundable for a period that has
already started, except in these cases:

- **Charged in error.** If we charge you twice, charge the wrong amount, or charge you after you
  cancelled in time, we will refund the amount charged in error in full.
- **We do not provide the service.** If the service suffers a serious failure that stops your
  organisation using it, and we do not fix it within 30 days of you telling us, you may end the
  subscription and we will refund the fees you paid in advance for the period after it ends.
- **We end the service.** If we stop offering the service, or end your subscription for any reason
  other than your breach of the subscription agreement, we will refund the fees you paid in advance
  for the period after it ends.
- **You object to a new sub-processor.** If we cannot resolve a reasonable objection you raise
  under our [data processing agreement](/data-processing-agreement), we will refund the fees you
  paid in advance for the part of the subscription that ends.

## Changing your plan

If you upgrade or add seats part-way through a period, you pay the pro-rata difference for the rest
of that period. If you downgrade or remove seats, the change takes effect at the next renewal and
the current period is not refunded.

## How refunds are paid

We refund to the original payment method, within 14 days of agreeing the refund. The time it takes
to reach your account depends on your bank or card provider.

## Your other rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the law of England and Wales that cannot be
excluded by contract.

Questions about a payment or refund: [contact us](/contact).
