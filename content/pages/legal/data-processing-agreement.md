# Data processing agreement

This agreement forms part of the subscription agreement between Archiet Ltd ("we", "us") and the
organisation that subscribes to the hosted Entelim service ("you", "the customer"). It applies
whenever we process personal data on your behalf in providing that service, and it takes
precedence over the subscription agreement where the two conflict on data protection.

It is written to meet Article 28 of the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data
Protection Act 2018. Where you are subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation, it applies
in the same way to processing that falls under that regulation.

## 1. Roles

You are the controller of the personal data your users enter into, or import into, your
organisation's workspace. We are your processor for that data.

We are a controller, not a processor, for the limited data we need to run the relationship with
you: the contact and billing details of the people who buy and administer the subscription. Our
[privacy notice](/privacy) covers that data.

## 2. What we process, and why

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject matter** | Hosting and operating the Entelim enterprise architecture platform for your organisation. |
| **Duration** | The term of your subscription, plus the deletion period in section 10. |
| **Nature and purpose** | Storing, organising, retrieving, displaying, analysing and exporting the records your users create, so the service can answer their questions; authenticating users; sending service emails. |
| **Types of personal data** | User account data (name, work email address, role, sign-in and audit history) and any personal data your users choose to record in the workspace, such as the names of application owners, stakeholders or contacts at suppliers. |
| **Data subjects** | Your staff, contractors and other people you invite as users, and people named in the records your users create. |
| **Special category data** | None is needed to use the service. You agree not to record special category or criminal offence data in it. |

## 3. Instructions

We process your personal data only on your documented instructions. The subscription agreement,
this agreement, and your use and configuration of the service are your complete instructions at
the start. If we are required by UK law to process the data in some other way, we will tell you
first unless that law forbids it. If we believe an instruction breaks data protection law, we will
tell you promptly.

## 4. Confidentiality

Everyone we authorise to process your personal data is bound by a duty of confidentiality, whether
by contract or by law, and has access only to what their role requires.

## 5. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, as
Article 32 requires. They include:

- encryption of data in transit between users' browsers and the service;
- separation of each organisation's records, so one customer's users cannot read another's;
- passwords stored only as salted hashes, never in readable form;
- sessions that expire after a period of inactivity, and protection against cross-site request
  forgery on every action that changes data;
- role-based access within your workspace, controlled by your administrators;
- an audit trail of significant changes made in the workspace.

We review these measures as the service and the risks change, and we will not reduce the overall
level of protection during your subscription.

## 6. Sub-processors

You authorise us to use sub-processors to provide the service. They fall into these categories:

- cloud hosting and infrastructure, for running the service and storing its data;
- transactional email delivery, for password resets and notifications;
- payment processing, for taking subscription payments;
- AI model providers, only where your organisation uses the AI features of the service.

We will give you our current list of sub-processors on request. We will tell you at least 30 days
before we add or replace a sub-processor. If you object on reasonable data protection grounds, we
will discuss it with you in good faith; if we cannot resolve it, you may end the affected part of
the subscription and we will refund any fees you have paid in advance for the period after it ends.

Each sub-processor is bound by written terms that protect your personal data at least as well as
this agreement does. We remain responsible to you for what our sub-processors do.

## 7. International transfers

We will not transfer your personal data outside the United Kingdom unless the transfer is covered by
UK adequacy regulations or by appropriate safeguards, such as the International Data Transfer
Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any additional
measures the transfer needs.

## 8. Helping you meet your obligations

Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to us, we will:

- help you respond to requests from people exercising their data protection rights — much of this
  you can do yourself in the service, and where you cannot, we will help. If a request reaches us
  directly, we will pass it to you and not respond to it ourselves unless you ask us to;
- help you with your security obligations, with data protection impact assessments, and with prior
  consultation of the Information Commissioner's Office where that is needed.

## 9. Personal data breaches

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your personal data, we will tell you without
undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours. We will give you the information you need to meet
your own reporting obligations, as it becomes available, and take reasonable steps to contain the
breach and limit its effects.

## 10. When the subscription ends

When your subscription ends, you can export your data for 30 days. After that, we will delete your
personal data from the live service, unless UK law requires us to keep it. Copies in backups are
deleted as the backups expire on their normal cycle, within 90 days, and are protected by this
agreement until then.

## 11. Information and audits

We will make available the information you reasonably need to show that this agreement is being
met. We will also allow audits by you, or by an independent auditor you appoint who is bound by
confidentiality, on at least 30 days' notice, no more than once a year unless a regulator requires
it or a personal data breach has occurred. Audits take place during business hours and must not
disrupt the service for other customers. Each party bears its own costs.

## 12. Liability and law

Each party's liability under this agreement is subject to the limits in the subscription
agreement. This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and
Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
