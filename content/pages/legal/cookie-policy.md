# Cookie policy

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies Archiet Ltd uses on the Entelim website
and hosted service, as the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations require. Our
[privacy notice](/privacy) explains how we handle personal data more generally.

## The short version

We use only the cookies the service needs to work: to keep you signed in and to protect your
account. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not use analytics cookies. Because these
cookies are strictly necessary, the law does not require us to ask for your consent to them, but we
still want you to know what they are.

## Cookies we set

| Cookie | What it does | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| `session` | Keeps you signed in as you move between pages, and holds a security token that stops other websites from making changes in your account without your knowledge. | Ends after 30 minutes without activity, and after 8 hours at most. |
| `remember_token` | Keeps you signed in between visits. Set only if you tick "Remember me" when you sign in. | 30 days, renewed while you keep using the service. Signing out removes it. |

Both cookies are sent only to our own service, cannot be read by scripts on the page, and are not
used to track you across other websites.

## Preferences stored in your browser

Some screens remember your choices — light or dark theme, whether the sidebar is collapsed, the
width of panels, saved filters, and unsaved drafts — using your browser's local storage. These
stay on your device, are not sent to us, and exist only because you asked the page to remember
something. You can clear them at any time in your browser's settings.

## Payments

When you pay for a subscription, you complete the payment on pages provided by our payment
processor, Stripe. Stripe sets its own cookies there, mainly to prevent fraud, and its own cookie
policy applies to them.

## If this changes

If we ever want to use a cookie that is not strictly necessary — for example, to measure how the
service is used — we will ask for your consent before setting it, and we will update this page
first.

## Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. If you block the cookies above, you will
not be able to sign in to the service.

Questions about cookies: [contact us](/contact).
