# Know what your startup runs and who owns it, from the day you connect GitHub

A startup runs on more than anyone has written down: a handful of services, a few vendors, and the people who know how they fit together. When an investor, a customer's security team or an outage asks what depends on what, the answer lives in someone's head. Entelim starts from the tools your engineers already use. Sign up free and connect GitHub or Jira: Entelim proposes your services, who owns them and what depends on what, and nothing joins your model until a person approves it. Then ask the AI chat what breaks if a service fails and read an answer that names the records behind it.

**[Start free](/register)** or **[see pricing](/pricing)**.

## What a founder needs to know

- **What do we run, and who owns each piece?** Entelim reads your repositories and proposes the services and their owners, each traced to the repository, commit and file it came from.
- **What breaks if this fails?** Ask the [AI chat](/modules/ai-chat) in plain words. The answer lists what depends on the service and cites the record behind each line, with a record to open.
- **Can I show an investor what we run?** The [twin map](/modules/digital-twin) draws the same model as a picture you can share.
- **How does the business fit together?** Fill in a business model canvas in the [business model canvas](/modules/business-model-canvas) and keep it beside the systems that carry it.

## What you do, step by step

1. Sign up free and connect GitHub or Jira. Both are part of sign-up on every plan.
2. Review the services Entelim found and approve the ones that are right.
3. Ask the AI chat what breaks if one of them fails. Open the records beneath the answer to check it.
4. Invite the two people who most need the answer. The free Community plan covers up to three people.

## Questions founders ask

### Do I need an architecture model first?

No. The quickest start is connecting GitHub or Jira. You can also import a spreadsheet or an ArchiMate file, or record applications by hand.

### What does it cost?

Community is free for one organisation of up to three people, with no trial clock. The Startup plan is for a team of ten. [See full pricing](/pricing).

### Can my AI assistant use it?

Yes. Connect your own AI assistant from your account, on every plan. It reads your model and cites the records, and any change it suggests waits in your approval queue.
