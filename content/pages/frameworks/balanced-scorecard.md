# Balanced Scorecard in Entelim: four views, one strategy

The Balanced Scorecard, from Robert Kaplan and David Norton, turns a strategy into measures across four perspectives: financial, customer, internal processes, and learning and growth. Its point is balance: a company that watches only financial results misses what produces them. A scorecard states the vision and strategy, then the measures, the targets and the initiatives that serve them.

## Who uses the Balanced Scorecard, and when

The Balanced Scorecard suits an executive team that wants strategy translated into a handful of measures a whole organisation can follow. It is used in larger organisations, public bodies and charities as well as companies, wherever financial results alone say too little. The test of a good scorecard is that each measure is something a person can influence, and each initiative on it is real work that is funded.

## How Entelim opens the Balanced Scorecard at sign-up

Tick Balanced Scorecard on the company step of sign-up and Entelim creates it once for your organisation and fills each box that takes elements with elements already in your model. If you skip it, open Canvases from the sidebar at any time and choose Balanced Scorecard from the list of canvas types. The boxes are vision, strategy, the financial, customer, internal and learning perspectives, measures, targets and initiatives. A canvas that exists is left as you have it, so choosing it again never overwrites your work.

## What you get

- The Balanced Scorecard with its boxes (vision, strategy, the financial, customer, internal and learning perspectives, measures, targets and initiatives), each saved on its own as you work.
- Boxes that take elements from your model, so you choose what already exists and name only what is new.
- One model beneath all your canvases: an element used here appears wherever else it belongs, and is changed once.
- An export of the canvas as an image, for the person who asks to see it.
- Where AI is enabled, a draft button for a single box. The draft is advice only: nothing is written until you edit and save it.

## How to use it

1. Open Canvases from the sidebar and choose Balanced Scorecard.
2. Write the vision and strategy in a sentence or two each.
3. For each of the four perspectives, set the objective that carries the strategy.
4. Give each objective a measure and a target you can actually collect.
5. List the initiatives that will move each measure, and link them to the portfolio.

## Why Entelim is different

Most canvases are drawn once in a slide or a whiteboard tool and never opened again. In Entelim a canvas is a view of your model: each box takes elements that already exist, so the same customer segment, goal or application appears wherever it is used and changes in one place. Connect GitHub or Jira at sign-up and the model starts from the repositories and projects you already have. Where AI is enabled, a draft button proposes text for a single box from the others, and every answer in the AI chat cites the records behind it. Initiatives on the scorecard are the ones in your portfolio, so a measure has real work under it and the work has a measure above it.
