# OKR in Entelim: objectives and key results with initiatives

Objectives and Key Results, popularised by John Doerr, is a way of setting goals. An objective says what you want in plain words; its key results are a few measurable outcomes that show it was reached. Initiatives are the work you will do to get there. OKRs are set for a period and reviewed openly, so they work best with few objectives and honest scoring.

## Who uses the OKR, and when

OKRs suit a team that wants to focus on a few outcomes for a quarter or a year and review them openly. They are used by start-ups and large companies alike, and by product, engineering and leadership teams. The common mistake is writing key results as tasks. A key result is a number you could be wrong about. Initiatives are the tasks, and they belong in a separate box.

## How Entelim opens the OKR at sign-up

Tick OKR on the company step of sign-up and Entelim creates it once for your organisation and fills each box that takes elements with elements already in your model. If you skip it, open Canvases from the sidebar at any time and choose OKR from the list of canvas types. The boxes are objective, key results and initiatives. A canvas that exists is left as you have it, so choosing it again never overwrites your work.

## What you get

- The OKR with its boxes (objective, key results and initiatives), each saved on its own as you work.
- Boxes that take elements from your model, so you choose what already exists and name only what is new.
- One model beneath all your canvases: an element used here appears wherever else it belongs, and is changed once.
- An export of the canvas as an image, for the person who asks to see it.
- Where AI is enabled, a draft button for a single box. The draft is advice only: nothing is written until you edit and save it.

## How to use it

1. Open Canvases from the sidebar and choose OKR.
2. Write the objective as something a person could be excited by, in a sentence.
3. Add two to five key results, each a number with a starting point and a target.
4. List the initiatives that you will do to move those numbers.
5. Review at the end of the period and carry forward only what still matters.

## Why Entelim is different

Most canvases are drawn once in a slide or a whiteboard tool and never opened again. In Entelim a canvas is a view of your model: each box takes elements that already exist, so the same customer segment, goal or application appears wherever it is used and changes in one place. Connect GitHub or Jira at sign-up and the model starts from the repositories and projects you already have. Where AI is enabled, a draft button proposes text for a single box from the others, and every answer in the AI chat cites the records behind it. Initiatives are the work packages in your portfolio, so an objective's work is visible on the roadmap and the roadmap's work is tied to an objective.
