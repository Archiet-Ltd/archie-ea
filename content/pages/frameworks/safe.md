# SAFe in Entelim: portfolio Kanban on your own work

The Scaled Agile Framework, SAFe, is a set of practices for planning and delivering work across many agile teams. At the portfolio level it uses a Kanban of states through which an epic, a large initiative, moves from an idea to a finished piece of work. Entelim holds those states and the model of your own work next to them. Certification in SAFe is awarded to individuals by Scaled Agile, Inc.; Entelim gives the people who hold it a model to work on.

## Who uses SAFe, and when

SAFe suits larger organisations that run many agile teams against shared goals, where funding and priorities are decided above the team. The portfolio Kanban is the part that governs which large initiatives are funded and in what order. It is also used by smaller companies that borrow its vocabulary. Whether you follow the whole framework or only its portfolio board, the problem is the same: the board shows state, but not what an epic changes. Entelim connects each initiative to the applications and capabilities it reaches, so the board has consequences attached.

## How Entelim loads SAFe at sign-up

Tick SAFe on the company step of sign-up, or adopt it later in Settings, and Entelim adopts the framework with its own list: the portfolio Kanban states Funnel, Reviewing, Analyzing, Portfolio Backlog, Implementing and Done, each described by the column it occupies. They appear on the Frameworks page and the compliance dashboard. Your epics, work packages and initiatives live in the same model as your applications, so a state belongs to something real.

## What you get

- The six portfolio Kanban states listed with what each means.
- Initiatives in a portfolio, with budget, spend to date, health and sponsor.
- Work packages and roadmaps, so an epic in Implementing can be traced to the changes that deliver it.
- Jira projects brought in at sign-up, so the work you already track is where the model starts.

## How to use it

1. Choose SAFe at sign-up, or adopt it from Settings.
2. Connect Jira on the sources step if your epics live there, and let the model start from the projects it finds.
3. Register your initiatives in the portfolio and place each in the Kanban state it is in today.
4. Link each initiative to the applications and capabilities it changes.
5. Review the portfolio together: which epics are waiting, which are being implemented and what each is costing.

## Common mistakes

The usual mistakes are moving epics between states without a decision, and letting the Kanban fill with ideas nobody will fund. A state means something only if entering it requires something: an analysis, a sponsor, a cost. Limit the number in each state and review the board regularly.

## Why Entelim is different

Portfolio Kanban fails when the board is disconnected from what the epics change. In Entelim an initiative is linked to the applications and capabilities it touches, so the board answers what a change reaches. Connect Jira at sign-up and the model starts from the projects you already track; connect GitHub and it starts from your repositories. Every answer the AI chat gives cites its records.
