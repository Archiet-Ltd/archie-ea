# SWOT analysis in Entelim: four boxes, linked evidence

SWOT analysis sorts what matters to an organisation into four boxes: strengths and weaknesses, which are internal, and opportunities and threats, which are external. It is among the oldest planning tools and its weakness is vagueness: a list of adjectives helps nobody. Used well, each entry is specific enough to act on.

## Who uses the SWOT, and when

SWOT suits the start of a planning conversation: a strategy round, a new market, a reorganisation. It is quick to run in a workshop and everyone understands it. Its limit is that it describes and does not decide, so the useful part is what you do next: turn the strongest weaknesses and threats into work and the best opportunities into goals. Pair it with PESTLE when the outside view needs more depth.

## How to use it

1. Open Canvases from the sidebar and choose SWOT.
2. Write strengths and weaknesses about the organisation itself, each specific enough to point at something.
3. Write opportunities and threats about the outside: markets, regulation, competitors, suppliers.
4. Pick existing assessments from the model where they exist, so the same finding is not typed twice.
5. Choose the two or three entries that most deserve action and take them to a roadmap or the review board.
