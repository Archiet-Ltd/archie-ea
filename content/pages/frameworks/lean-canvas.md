# Lean Canvas: test an idea in twelve boxes in Entelim

The Lean Canvas, adapted from the Business Model Canvas by Ash Maurya, is built for a business that has not found its footing yet. It starts from the problem and the alternatives people use today, then asks for a solution, key metrics, a unique value proposition, a high-level concept, an unfair advantage, channels, customer segments, early adopters, cost structure and revenue streams. It is a set of hypotheses to test, not a plan.

## Who uses the Lean Canvas, and when

The Lean Canvas suits a founder or a new product team that has an idea and needs to find out whether anyone will pay for it. It forces the uncomfortable questions first: is the problem real, what do people do about it today, who would buy first. It is deliberately quick, and a first version can be filled in within an hour. The skill is in being specific enough to be wrong, because a box that cannot be disproved cannot teach you anything.

## How Entelim opens the Lean Canvas at sign-up

If you skip it, open Canvases from the sidebar at any time and choose Lean Canvas from the list of canvas types. The boxes are problem, existing alternatives, solution, key metrics, unique value proposition, high-level concept, unfair advantage, channels, customer segments, early adopters, cost structure and revenue streams. A canvas that exists is left as you have it, so choosing it again never overwrites your work.

## What you get

- The Lean Canvas with its boxes (problem, existing alternatives, solution, key metrics, unique value proposition, high-level concept, unfair advantage, channels, customer segments, early adopters, cost structure and revenue streams), each saved on its own as you work.
- Boxes that take elements from your model, so you choose what already exists and name only what is new.
- One model beneath all your canvases: an element used here appears wherever else it belongs, and is changed once.
- An export of the canvas as an image, for the person who asks to see it.
- Where AI is enabled, a draft button for a single box. The draft is advice only: nothing is written until you edit and save it.

## How to use it

1. Open Canvases from the sidebar and choose Lean Canvas.
2. Write the problem and the existing alternatives first, before any solution.
3. Name the customer segments and, inside them, the early adopters you will approach first.
4. Add the solution, the key metrics that would tell you it works, and your unique value proposition.
5. Review it after each round of customer conversations and change only the boxes that the evidence moved.
