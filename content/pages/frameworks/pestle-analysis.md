# PESTLE analysis: six outside forces, one model

PESTLE analysis scans the external environment of an organisation in six categories: political, economic, social, technological, legal and environmental. It answers a narrower question than SWOT: what is happening around us that we do not control, but must plan for. It is most useful before a strategy round, a market entry or a long investment.

## Who uses the PESTLE, and when

PESTLE suits an organisation planning beyond the next quarter, entering a new market or operating in a regulated sector. It forces attention onto factors that arrive from outside: a new regulation, an interest rate, a change in how people behave, a technology shift. It is most valuable when each entry names the source and the consequence for you. Run it before a SWOT so that opportunities and threats are grounded in something real.

## How to use it

1. Open Canvases from the sidebar and choose PESTLE.
2. Go through the six boxes in turn and write only factors that affect your organisation, not general news.
3. Prefer entries with a source you can name, such as a regulation, a price index or a published standard.
4. Mark which factors are drivers of change and link them to the goals they affect.
5. Take the factors that matter into a SWOT, where they become opportunities and threats.
