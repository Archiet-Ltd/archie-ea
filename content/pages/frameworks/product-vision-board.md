# Product Vision Board in Entelim: vision to business goals

The Product Vision Board, from Roman Pichler, is a one-page tool for agreeing what a product is for before building it. It asks for a vision, the target group, the needs the product meets, the product itself and the business goals it serves. Its value is in the conversation: a board filled in by one person is a guess, and one filled in by the team is a plan to test.

## Who uses the Product Vision Board, and when

The Product Vision Board suits a product team at the start of a product or a change in its direction, and a leader who needs the team to share one picture. It is lighter than the Lean Canvas and is often used first, before the detail is known. The conversation matters more than the page: do not fill it in alone and present it, but fill it in together and argue over it.

## What you get

- The Product Vision Board with its boxes (vision, target group, needs, product and business goals), each saved on its own as you work.
- Boxes that take elements from your model, so you choose what already exists and name only what is new.
- One model beneath all your canvases: an element used here appears wherever else it belongs, and is changed once.
- An export of the canvas as an image, for the person who asks to see it.
- Where AI is enabled, a draft button for a single box. The draft is advice only: nothing is written until you edit and save it.

## How to use it

1. Open Canvases from the sidebar and choose Product Vision Board.
2. State the vision as the reason the product should exist, in a sentence.
3. Name the target group and the needs you will meet for them.
4. Describe the product at the level of what it does for those needs, not its features.
5. Write the business goals it serves, and check they match your Business Model Canvas.
