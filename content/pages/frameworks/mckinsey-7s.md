# McKinsey 7S in Entelim: seven elements of alignment

The McKinsey 7S model, from consultants at McKinsey and Company, looks at an organisation through seven elements that must fit together: strategy, structure, systems, shared values, skills, style and staff. Its premise is that a change fails when one element moves and the others do not. It is used before a reorganisation, a merger or a large technology change.

## Who uses the McKinsey 7S, and when

The McKinsey 7S model suits a leadership team that is about to change something large and wants to know what else will have to move. A new strategy needs matching skills; a new structure needs matching systems. It is used in mergers, reorganisations and transformation programmes, and works best as a group exercise in which the soft elements are discussed honestly. Its output is a list of misfits, each of which becomes work.

## How to use it

1. Open Canvases from the sidebar and choose McKinsey 7S.
2. Fill the hard elements first: strategy, structure and systems, with specifics.
3. Fill the soft elements: shared values, skills, style and staff, from what people actually do.
4. Look for misfits, for example a strategy that needs skills nobody has.
5. Turn each misfit into a work package and place it on a roadmap.
