# Business Model Canvas: nine blocks on your own model

The Business Model Canvas, devised by Alexander Osterwalder, describes a business on one page in nine blocks: customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key activities, key resources, key partners and cost structure. It suits a company that wants to talk about its model without writing a document. It is a thinking tool: a canvas states a view, and your results prove it.

## Who uses the Business Model Canvas, and when

The Business Model Canvas suits a founder explaining a business to investors, a product leader checking that a new line makes sense, or an executive team aligning on what the company actually sells and to whom. Its strength is that it fits on one page and everyone can challenge a block. It is less suited to testing an unproven idea from scratch, where the Lean Canvas is a better start. A good canvas is specific: named segments, a real price, partners you can name.

## What you get

- The Business Model Canvas with its boxes (customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key activities, key resources, key partners and cost structure), each saved on its own as you work.
- Boxes that take elements from your model, so you choose what already exists and name only what is new.
- One model beneath all your canvases: an element used here appears wherever else it belongs, and is changed once.
- An export of the canvas as an image, for the person who asks to see it.
- Where AI is enabled, a draft button for a single box. The draft is advice only: nothing is written until you edit and save it.

## How to use it

1. Open Canvases from the sidebar and choose Business Model Canvas.
2. Start with customer segments and value propositions, picking existing elements where they exist and naming new ones where they do not.
3. Work across to channels, relationships and revenue streams, and price a revenue stream where the canvas allows it.
4. Fill key activities, resources and partners from the applications, capabilities and vendors already in the model.
5. Export the canvas as an image when someone asks to see it.
