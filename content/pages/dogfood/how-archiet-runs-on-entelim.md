# How Archiet Ltd runs on Entelim

*Archiet is Entelim's first customer — not a demo account, the company itself.*

## Why this page exists

Most vendors show you a demo tenant built to look good. This page shows the real one: Archiet Ltd's
own operating model, the same twin the team uses to make real decisions, with real projects traced
to real gaps.

## What we actually ask it

When we need to know who to hire next, what to build, or what to stop, we ask Entelim — not a
general assistant with no knowledge of our company. Every answer comes from our own twin, reasoned
over our own data, not a guess dressed up as advice.

## Every project traces to a gap and a stage

A project on our roadmap isn't just a line item. It's linked to the specific gap it closes and the
stage it moves us toward — the same traceability every Entelim customer gets, running against our
own company first.

## Why we built it this way

Entelim's founder is an enterprise architect and a startup founder — building this company on the
same model Entelim gives every customer. If it isn't good enough to run Archiet on, it isn't good
enough to ship.
